About

My path into IT wasn't a straight line — and that's exactly what I bring to it.

Since arriving in France in 2019, I've worked across retail, large-scale distribution, emergency services, social work, and healthcare before finding my place in technology. Different worlds on paper, but every one of them came down to the same thing: helping people, often under pressure, often when something had gone wrong. That's the lens I carry into systems engineering today.

Because for me, IT has never been only technical — it's a relationship business. Infrastructure is the nervous system of a company: invisible when it works, paralysing when it doesn't. And behind every system there's a person who just wants to get back to their work. So I listen before I diagnose, I design for the people who'll actually use what I build, and I document everything so the solution outlives me.

Today I'm an IT Systems Engineer at Contentsquare, where I architect and maintain the infrastructure behind a global device fleet — managing lifecycles, security compliance, and reliability at scale — and, whenever I hit a problem worth solving once and for all, I build the tools and automation to fix it for good.

What drives me is the balance at the heart of this craft: enough rigour to keep systems secure and reliable, enough empathy to keep them human. Done right, technology should quietly disappear into the background and simply let people do their best work.
